Problems found

These did not behave the way the product documents say they should. “High priority” means it affects customers’ money or a compliance requirement.

High priority

1. The main audit log is empty

Area: Investment approvals, alerts and audit log · Who should look at this: Engineering and Compliance

The portal is not keeping its official record of who did what. This is a compliance requirement.

What we saw
The Audit Log page says “No system actions have been recorded yet” and shows 0 entries for the past week — even though admin actions were carried out during this test.
What should happen
Every admin action should be recorded permanently in the audit log (Admin PRD). Entries were visible here on 21 October; it has been empty on 26 October and 2 November.
High priority

2. Loans on the same setting are repaid using different interest calculations

Area: Loan interest calculations · Who should look at this: Engineering (loan calculation) and Product

Customers on the same loan product are charged interest in different ways, and older loans may be charged the wrong amount. This affects money and must be confirmed before loans go live.

What we saw
Both loans are Personal loans at 8.5% set to “Monthly equal”. The newer loan #188 (₦500,000 over 9 months) collects ₦98,056 a month — the specification’s amount at 8.5% a month (₦98,055.56), rounded up to the whole naira. The older loan #169 (₦5,000,000 over 24 months) still collects ₦226,041.66 a month, which is what you get if 8.5% were the interest for the whole 24 months.
What should happen
Every loan should follow the same rule from the loan specification. For loan #169 that is ₦633,333.33 a month if 8.5% is a monthly rate, or ₦243,750.00 if it is a yearly rate (loan #188: ₦98,055.56 or ₦59,097.23). Rounding up to the whole naira also means loan #188 would collect ₦4 more than the schedule over 9 months unless the last payment is adjusted.
High priority

3. 7.5% “Loan VAT” is taken from every loan payout without being shown on the loan

Area: Loan interest calculations · Who should look at this: Engineering (loans) and Finance

Customers receive less than they borrowed, and neither the loan record nor the offer explains why. It happens on every loan checked, including new ones.

What we saw
All three loans show a management fee of ₦0, yet exactly 7.5% was kept back: #169 ₦5,000,000 → ₦4,625,000 paid out; #188 ₦500,000 → ₦462,500; #189 (paid out during this round) ₦100,000 → ₦92,500. The customer’s wallet history shows the full amount credited, then the 7.5% taken back as “Loan VAT”.
What should happen
The amount paid out should equal the loan amount minus the charges shown on the loan. Finance should confirm whether VAT applies to the amount lent (it normally applies to fees); if it does, it should be shown on the offer and the loan record.
Medium priority

4. Viewing a customer’s private details is not recorded

Area: Customer management · Who should look at this: Engineering and Compliance (data protection)

There is no record of which admin looked at a customer’s BVN or NIN, which data-protection rules expect.

What we saw
After revealing a customer’s hidden BVN/NIN, the customer’s Audit tab still says “No audit entries found”.
What should happen
Each time an admin reveals private details, it should appear in that customer’s audit history.
Medium priority

5. A new biller disappears after it is submitted

Area: Transactions, billers and communications · Who should look at this: Engineering (Transactions & Ledger)

Admins cannot add new bill-payment providers, and they get no message telling them anything went wrong.

What we saw
After filling in a new biller and pressing “Submit for approval”, it does not appear in the billers list or in either approvals tab, and no message is shown.
What should happen
The new biller should wait in the approvals inbox for a second admin to approve it, as it did on 21 October.

Differences from the documents (not failures)

These work, but don’t match what the specifications say. Product should confirm which is correct.

  • RWIN holding period. When booking an RWIN plan for a customer, the form asks them to accept a 1-day minimum holding period. The RWIN 2.0 specification says 30 days, with no exceptions.
  • Early-repayment charge on loans. Personal and Public Sector loan products have a “Liquidation charge” (3% by default). The loan specification says customers can repay early without any penalty.
  • Personal loan limits. A new Personal loan product starts at ₦50,000–₦6,000,000 and 3–18 months. The specification allows up to ₦30,000,000 and 3–24 months. (These can be changed by an admin.)
  • Daily-interest loans. Nano and Payday loans can be switched to a monthly interest model in the admin portal, although the specification says they are always charged per day.

Questions for the product team

  • Is the loan “Interest rate” shown in the portal a monthly rate (as in the specification’s 2% examples) or a yearly rate? Newer loans now treat it as monthly; loan #169 matches neither.
  • Should monthly loan repayments be rounded to the whole naira, and if so, is the difference corrected in the last payment?
  • Should existing loans (such as #169) be recalculated under the new interest rule, or keep their original schedule?
  • What is the minimum customer verification level (KYC tier) for a Personal loan? The specification does not say; the tests assume Tier 2.

2. Send Money shows recipients the customer never added

Area: Account · Who should look at this: Engineering (mobile app) and Compliance
